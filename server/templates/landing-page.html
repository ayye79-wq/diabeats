Why DiabEats Exists

Americans live with diabetes — and tens of millions more have prediabetes. Eating out is one of the hardest challenges they face.

Restaurant menus rarely explain how meals affect blood sugar. Most people guess — and guessing costs them their health. DiabEats changes that.

"We built DiabEats so that a person with diabetes can walk into any restaurant, open the app, and instantly know what to order — with confidence."
