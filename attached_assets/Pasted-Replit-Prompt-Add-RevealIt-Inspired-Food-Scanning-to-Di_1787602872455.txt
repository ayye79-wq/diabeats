Replit Prompt — Add RevealIt-Inspired Food Scanning to Diabeats

Upgrade the existing Diabeats app by expanding the current BioTrace feature into a diabetes-focused food and product scanner.

Do not redesign the whole app. Keep the current Diabeats branding, colors, navigation style, authentication, and existing working features intact. The goal is to add a useful scanning workflow without breaking current functionality.

Core Feature

Create a new BioTrace experience where users can scan or photograph:

* A packaged food
* A nutrition facts label
* An ingredient list
* A product barcode, if barcode support already exists or can be added cleanly

The app should extract available nutrition information and provide a diabetes-focused explanation.

Scan Result Screen

After a successful scan, display:

Product Name

Show detected product name and brand when available.

Blood Sugar Fit Score

Show a simple score from 0–100 labeled:

Blood Sugar Fit

The score should be based primarily on factors such as:

* Total carbohydrates
* Fiber
* Added sugar
* Serving size
* Protein
* Sugar alcohols where relevant
* Overall carbohydrate density

Do not present this score as medical advice or claim that a food is universally good or bad.

Quick Summary

Generate a short plain-language explanation such as:

Moderate-carb choice. One serving contains 23 g of carbohydrates, 4 g of fiber, and 6 g of added sugar. Portion size may have a meaningful effect on blood glucose.

Nutrition Highlights

Clearly display:

* Calories
* Total carbohydrates
* Fiber
* Total sugar
* Added sugar
* Protein
* Sodium
* Serving size

Only show values that were successfully detected.

Ingredient Insights

Highlight ingredients that may matter to someone managing blood glucose, such as:

* Added sugars
* Syrups
* Refined starches
* Maltodextrin
* High-carbohydrate fillers
* Sugar alcohols
* High-fiber ingredients

For every flagged ingredient, explain why it matters.

Avoid fear-based words such as:

* Toxic
* Poison
* Dangerous
* Harmful

unless there is verified regulatory evidence requiring such language.

Better Choice Feature

Add a button:

Find a Better Choice

For the first version, this can generate general suggestions based on the scanned product category.

Example:

Look for a cereal with less added sugar, more fiber, and fewer carbohydrates per serving.

Do not invent specific products unless the app has reliable product data.

Compare Products

Allow users to save scanned products and compare two products side by side.

Comparison fields should include:

* Blood Sugar Fit score
* Carbs
* Fiber
* Added sugar
* Protein
* Serving size

Highlight which option appears more glucose-friendly based on the available nutrition information.

Scan History

Create a BioTrace scan history showing:

* Product name
* Date scanned
* Blood Sugar Fit score
* Thumbnail if available

Allow users to reopen previous scans.

Favorites

Allow users to save frequently used products to Favorites.

Future-Ready Glucose Response Section

Add the UI structure for a future feature called:

My Glucose Response

Do not fabricate glucose readings.

For now, show a placeholder such as:

When glucose tracking is connected, Diabeats will help you understand how foods you eat relate to your glucose patterns.

Design the data model so a future product scan can be linked to:

* Meal timestamp
* Pre-meal glucose
* Post-meal glucose
* Glucose change
* Time to peak

Do not implement insulin dosing recommendations.

Safety

Diabeats must remain informational and educational.

Add appropriate wording making clear that:

* The score is not a medical diagnosis.
* Food responses vary between individuals.
* Users should follow guidance from their healthcare professional.
* The app must not recommend insulin doses or medication changes.

BioTrace Positioning

Update the BioTrace area to use this positioning:

BioTrace

Scan. Understand. Choose.

Supporting copy:

Scan nutrition labels and ingredients to understand how a food may fit into your diabetes management.

UX Requirements

Keep the workflow simple:

BioTrace → Scan → Analyze → Results → Save / Compare

The scan screen should include:

* Take Photo
* Choose Photo
* Scan Barcode, if supported

The result screen should feel visual and easy to understand, not like a medical report.

Use the existing Diabeats color palette and component styles.

Technical Requirements

Before modifying anything:

1. Inspect the existing Diabeats codebase.
2. Identify the current BioTrace implementation.
3. Reuse existing components, services, database structure, camera permissions, authentication, and styling where possible.
4. Do not duplicate functionality already present.
5. Do not change unrelated screens.
6. Do not break the existing Android or iOS build.
7. Keep Android media permissions compliant with Google Play policy. Do not reintroduce READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or READ_MEDIA_VIDEO for occasional image selection. Continue using the system Photo Picker or equivalent compliant mechanism.
8. Camera capture should request only the camera permission actually needed.
9. Handle failed OCR, missing nutrition values, and incomplete scans gracefully.
